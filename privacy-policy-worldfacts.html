Privacy Policy – WorldFacts

Last updated: September 2026  |  Contact: plexoid.labs@gmail.com

Overview

WorldFacts ("the App") is a mobile application that allows users to explore countries, learn facts, and test their knowledge through quizzes. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Account Information (Google Sign-In)

When you sign in with Google, we receive:

  • Display name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo URL

This information is stored locally on your device using secure storage and is used to personalize your experience.

Quiz and Leaderboard Data

When you play quizzes, we store:

  • Quiz scores and statistics (locally on your device)
  • Leaderboard entries (on Firebase Firestore) including: user ID, display name, profile photo URL, quiz scores, and timestamps

Leaderboard data is publicly visible to other users of the App.

Subscriptions (WorldFacts Plus)

WorldFacts Plus subscriptions are purchased through Google Play and managed with RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and your subscription status and purchase history. Payments are processed by Google Play: we never receive your payment card details.

Crash and Error Reports

To detect and fix problems, the App sends crash and error reports to Sentry. A report may include:

  • Device model, operating system version, language and time zone
  • App version and technical state of the device at the time of the error (e.g. available memory, battery level, network type)
  • A random identifier of the app installation
  • A log of the actions in the App that led to the error
  • Approximate country, derived from the IP address (the IP address itself is not stored)

Reports do not include your name, email address, or Google account. They are stored in Sentry's EU data region (Germany).

Country Data and Images

The App downloads country data files and loads flag, coat-of-arms, and Wikipedia content from third-party servers (see Third-Party Services). Like any web request, these servers receive your IP address. No account information is sent to them.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • The App does not show advertisements and does not use your advertising ID for advertising or tracking
  • We do not collect precise location data
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or other device data
  • We do not sell any personal information to third parties

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and personalize your experience
  • To display your quiz scores on the leaderboard
  • To manage WorldFacts Plus subscriptions
  • To detect and fix crashes and errors
  • To improve the App experience

Quiz reminders and achievement notifications, if enabled, are generated locally on your device: no data is sent for them.

Data Storage

  • Local data: App settings, quiz history, downloaded country data, and session tokens are stored on your device using AsyncStorage and SecureStore. This data is deleted when you log out or uninstall the App.
  • Cloud data: Leaderboard entries are stored in Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud, europe-west1 region).

Data Retention

  • Local data is retained until you log out or uninstall the App
  • Leaderboard data is retained to maintain the global leaderboard
  • Crash and error reports are kept by Sentry for a limited period and then deleted automatically
  • You can request deletion of your leaderboard data by contacting us at plexoid.labs@gmail.com

Children's Privacy

WorldFacts is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we store about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Delete all local data at any time by logging out or uninstalling the App

To exercise these rights, contact us at plexoid.labs@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be communicated through App updates. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
