Privacy Policy – WorldFacts

Last updated: September 2026  |  Contact: plexoid.labs@gmail.com

Overview

WorldFacts ("the App") is a mobile application that allows users to explore countries, learn facts, and test their knowledge through quizzes. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Account Information (Google Sign-In)

When you sign in with Google, we receive:

  • Display name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo URL

This information is stored locally on your device using secure storage and is used to personalize your experience.

To protect the leaderboard, the App also signs you in to Firebase Authentication (Google) with the same Google account. Firebase Authentication stores your Google account ID, display name, email address and profile photo URL, so that only you can post scores under your name.

Quiz and Leaderboard Data

When you play quizzes, we store:

  • Quiz scores and statistics (locally on your device)
  • Leaderboard entries (on Firebase Firestore) including: user ID, display name, profile photo URL, quiz scores, and timestamps

Leaderboard data is publicly visible to other users of the App.

Subscriptions (WorldFacts Plus)

WorldFacts Plus subscriptions are purchased through Google Play and managed with RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and your subscription status and purchase history. Payments are processed by Google Play: we never receive your payment card details.

Crash and Error Reports

To detect and fix problems, the App sends crash and error reports to Sentry. A report may include:

  • Device model, operating system version, language and time zone
  • App version and technical state of the device at the time of the error (e.g. available memory, battery level, network type)
  • A random identifier of the app installation
  • A log of the actions in the App that led to the error
  • Approximate country, derived from the IP address (the IP address itself is not stored)

Reports do not include your name, email address, or Google account. They are stored in Sentry's EU data region (Germany).

Advertising (free version)

Users of the free version see ads served by Unity (Unity Technologies) through its LevelPlay platform. Before any ad is shown, the App asks you to choose:

  • Personalized ads: ads may be based on your interests
  • Non-personalized ads: ads are not based on your interests
  • I am under 16: no ads are shown and the advertising SDK is never started

Depending on your choice, Unity and the advertising partners it works with may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Device and network information (model, operating system, language, IP address)
  • Approximate location, derived from the IP address
  • Interactions with ads (views, clicks, rewarded videos watched)

With personalized ads this data may be used to show ads based on your interests. With non-personalized ads it is used only to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This data is processed by Unity according to the Unity Privacy Policy for app users.

WorldFacts Plus subscribers see no ads, and the advertising SDK is not started for them. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ads.

Country Data and Images

The App downloads country data files and loads flag, coat-of-arms, and Wikipedia content from third-party servers (see Third-Party Services). Like any web request, these servers receive your IP address. No account information is sent to them.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect precise location data
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or other device data
  • We do not sell any personal information to third parties

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and personalize your experience
  • To display your quiz scores on the leaderboard
  • To manage WorldFacts Plus subscriptions
  • To show ads to free users, according to your ad choice
  • To detect and fix crashes and errors
  • To improve the App experience

Quiz reminders and achievement notifications, if enabled, are generated locally on your device: no data is sent for them.

Data Storage

  • Local data: App settings, quiz history and statistics, downloaded country data, and session tokens are stored on your device using AsyncStorage and SecureStore. Session tokens, account information, quiz history and statistics are deleted when you log out. App settings (language, theme, notifications, ad choice), daily usage counters and downloaded country data stay on the device until you uninstall the App.
  • Cloud data: Leaderboard entries are stored in Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud, europe-west1 region). Your Firebase Authentication account is stored by Google Firebase.

Data Retention

  • Local data is retained as described in Data Storage
  • Leaderboard data and the Firebase Authentication account are retained to maintain the global leaderboard
  • Crash and error reports are kept by Sentry for a limited period and then deleted automatically
  • Advertising data is kept by Unity according to its own privacy policy
  • You can delete your account and data at any time (see Deleting Your Account)

Deleting Your Account

In the App: open Settings → Delete account and confirm. The App deletes:

  • all your scores on the leaderboard
  • your Firebase Authentication account
  • the link between WorldFacts and your Google account
  • your account data, quiz history and statistics on the device

App settings and daily usage counters stay on the device until you uninstall the App. A WorldFacts Plus subscription is not cancelled automatically: cancel it in the Google Play Store (Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). Crash reports (Sentry) and advertising data (Unity) do not contain your account and are deleted by those services according to their own retention periods.

Without the App: send an email to plexoid.labs@gmail.com from the address of your Google account, with the subject "Delete my WorldFacts account". We delete your leaderboard scores and your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children's Privacy

WorldFacts is not directed at children under the age of 13, and ads are never shown to users who declare they are under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we store about you
  • Delete your account and data (see Deleting Your Account)
  • Delete your account data and quiz history from the device by logging out, or all local data by uninstalling the App
  • Change or withdraw your ad consent at any time in Settings → Privacy & ads

To exercise these rights, contact us at plexoid.labs@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be communicated through App updates. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
